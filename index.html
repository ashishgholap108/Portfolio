About

The Editor

Hey, I'm Ashish. I'm a video editor focused on turning raw footage into engaging, polished content.

My journey started with learning video editing and motion graphics, followed by hands-on experience through professional training, an internship, full-time work, and independent projects.

I enjoy combining storytelling, pacing, motion, color, and sound to create edits that feel intentional and keep viewers engaged.

I'm continuously developing my skills across different formats, including talking-head, social content, long-form, commercial, and story-driven videos.
